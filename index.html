Dear Rumi 🩷

Among the countless faces this world has shown, yours became the one that felt like home. Not because you promised forever, but because your presence made ordinary moments brighter.

You may never know the quiet ways you've changed me how your smile lingered long after you were gone, how your words became comfort on difficult days, and how your kindness found places in me that needed healing.

I don't ask the stars to make you mine, nor do I expect life to follow my wishes. I simply hope that wherever your path may lead, happiness walks beside you every step of the way.

If someday our stories meet again, I'll be grateful for another chance to share a moment with you. And if they don't, I'll still cherish every memory we made, because some people become treasures not because we possess them, but because they remind us how beautiful it is to care.

You have become one of the brightest chapters of my life without even trying. Thank you for every smile, every laugh, and every moment that made my heart feel a little more alive.

So wherever life takes you, I hope it fills your days with peace, your heart with joy, and your dreams with endless possibilities. You deserve nothing less than a life as beautiful as the kindness you give to others.

You'll always have a special place in my heart.
Thank you for being someone I'll always hold dear. 🩷
